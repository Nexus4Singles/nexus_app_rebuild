## 🎯 The Core Strategy (60 Seconds)

### Problem
Filter-based dating search is overwhelming:
- 50-100+ profiles to browse
- No matching intelligence
- No context about who matches
- Feels generic (like Tinder/Bumble)
- Low conversion

### Solution: "Discover Daily"
Every morning, users get 6-10 **carefully curated, AI-matched profiles:**
- 97% compatibility shown (faith-weighted algorithm)
- Reasons they match explained
- Voice intros (not just photos)
- Intentional, not addictive

### The Algorithm
```
Compatibility Score = 
  (Core Values × 40%) +     ← Faith/tithing/tongues
  (Lifestyle × 35%) +       ← Marital status/kids/distance
  (Life Stage × 25%)        ← Age/location/timeline
Result: 0-100%
```

### The Experience
```
8:00 AM  → Open app, see 8 new matches
8:15 AM  → Browse all, save 2-3 favorites
8:20 AM  → Message 1 promising match
8:25 AM  → Back to life (not addicted)
8:00 AM (Tomorrow) → 8 fresh matches
```

### Why It Wins
- ✅ Only dating app that weights faith first
- ✅ Less choice paralysis (6-10 vs 100+)
- ✅ Better matches = higher conversion
- ✅ Daily refresh = daily habit
- ✅ Voice authenticity = real connections
